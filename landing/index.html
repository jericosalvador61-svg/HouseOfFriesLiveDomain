Our Story

One fry at a time,
since 1983.

What started as a small fries stand has grown into a Tagoloan favorite — the house of golden fries where families celebrate, students refuel, and every basket comes out hot, crispy and salted just right.

Today, our kitchen runs on a fully digital system: orders fire straight from your table's QR code to our screens, so all that's left to do is eat.
